Terra Bull Farm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Terra Bull Farm is a mobile application that provides farm-related information, activity tools, and other app features. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Data we collect

Terra Bull Farm does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information.

The app does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or similar tracking technologies.

The app does not collect or store user information on external servers. Information entered or created within the app is used only to provide the requested functionality and, where applicable, is stored locally on your device.

How your data is used

Information created within the app is used only for app functionality, such as displaying saved information, activity records, selections, or other locally stored content.

We do not sell personal information, use personal information for advertising, or track users across other apps or websites.

Local storage

Some app information may be stored locally on your device so that it remains available when you use the app again.

This locally stored information is not transmitted to our servers. You can remove locally stored information using available delete or reset functions within the app, or by removing the app from your device.

Device permissions

The app may request access to certain device features only when required for a specific function. Any permission granted is used only to provide the related feature and is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Third-party services

The app does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect information about users.

External services opened by the user, such as websites or system applications, are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's functionality or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
